# Privacy Policy

**Claude Usage (iOS / Android)**
Published by **MindZone**
Effective: 4 September 2026 · Last updated: 4 September 2026

---

## The short version

Claude Usage does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no
advertising, no tracking, and no server operated by MindZone. Everything the app
knows lives on your own device, and the only network request it makes is to a
Mac you own, on a network you control.

---

## 1. Who we are

Claude Usage is published by MindZone ("we", "us"). You can reach us at
**publish@appnest.tech**.

This policy covers the Claude Usage mobile app, including its Home Screen and
Lock Screen widgets and its Live Activity. It does not cover the separate
third-party macOS application the app pairs with (see §7).

## 2. Data we collect

**None.** We operate no servers, no accounts, and no back end. We cannot see
your usage figures, your device, your IP address, or the fact that you installed
the app at all.

In Apple's App Privacy terms, our declaration is **Data Not Collected**.

## 3. Data the app stores on your device

The app stores the following locally. It is never transmitted to us or to any
third party.

| What | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Usage history (token counts, timestamps, interval quality) | SQLite database in the app's sandbox | Every chart in the app is built from history the phone accumulates itself, because the Mac serves only a point-in-time snapshot |
| Pairing details (host, port, access token) | iOS Keychain / Android Keystore, via `expo-secure-store` | So you do not re-pair on every launch |
| Preferences (history retention, notification thresholds, units) | App preference storage | To remember your settings |
| The most recent snapshot | Shared App Group container | So the widgets and Live Activity can render without launching the app |

All of it is removed when you delete the app. History can also be cleared at any
time from **Settings → History**, and the pairing token is erased when you
unpair.

## 4. Network activity

The app makes network requests to exactly one destination: the host and port you
paired with, which is the local companion server running on **your own Mac**. It
is reached over your local network or your own private network such as Tailscale.

- Requests are `GET /v1/ping` and `GET /v1/usage`, authenticated with a Bearer
  token generated by your Mac.
- The app is read-only. It cannot change anything on the Mac.
- The app never contacts Anthropic, never sees or stores your Claude session key,
  and never authenticates to any Claude service. The session key stays on your
  Mac; only derived usage numbers cross your local network.
- There are no third-party SDKs, no crash reporters, no attribution or
  measurement libraries, and no remote configuration.

## 5. Permissions and why they are requested

**Camera** — used only to scan the pairing QR code displayed by the Mac app.
Frames are processed on-device to read the code and are never stored, recorded,
or transmitted. You can decline the permission entirely and pair by typing the
host, port and token manually.

**Local Network (iOS)** — required by iOS for the app to reach your Mac at a
local address. It is used solely for the paired host and port.

**Notifications** — optional. Notifications are generated locally on the device
(usage thresholds, session window reset, pace warnings). We operate no push
service and send you nothing.

**Background App Refresh** — optional. When iOS grants it, the app fetches a
snapshot from your Mac so history and widgets stay current while the app is
closed. It performs the same single request described in §4.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
information from anyone. Since we collect no data at all, there is nothing for us
to hold, delete, or disclose.

policy. Installing and enabling its companion server is your choice.

MindZone is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic PBC.
"Claude" is a trademark of Anthropic PBC.

## 8. Your rights

Because we collect and hold no personal data, there is no data for us to provide,
correct, export, or erase on request under GDPR, CCPA, or comparable laws. You
retain full control of the data on your device and can delete all of it by
clearing history in Settings or deleting the app.

## 9. Security

The pairing token is stored in the platform secure enclave-backed keystore
(iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). Communication with your Mac occurs over your
own local or private network. Because the companion server is intended for local
use, you should enable it only on networks you trust, and unpair from the Mac app
if a device is lost.
